Elisa already had the brand. What she didn't have was a site that
matched it. Every prior template she'd tried softened the identity into
something safer and more generic — exactly the thing the brand was
built to push back against.

So we cut the obvious. No stock photos. No pastel gradients. No
testimonial carousels. The flower mark from the brand kit became a
recurring motif across every page, the chunky display type sits as
large as it deserves, and the wine + chartreuse pairing carries the
whole site instead of being demoted to a button color.

The subscription pricing model is the part most agency sites won't
touch — recurring monthly retainers, three tiers, a real checkout
flow. We shipped it on day one and wired it to Stripe so Elisa can
flip it live the moment her first three retainers sign.
