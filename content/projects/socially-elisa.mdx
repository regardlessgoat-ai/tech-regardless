Elisa already had the brand. What she didn't have was a site that matched it. Every
prior template she'd tried softened the identity into something safer and more generic,
exactly the thing the brand was built to push back against.

So we cut the obvious. No stock photos, no pastel gradients, no testimonial carousels.
The flower mark from the brand kit became a recurring motif, the chunky display type sits
as large as it deserves, and the wine and chartreuse pairing carries the whole site instead
of being demoted to a button colour.
