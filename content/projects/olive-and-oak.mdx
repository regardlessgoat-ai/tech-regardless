The owners of Olive & Oak don't think of themselves as a "tech company" —
they think of themselves as cooks. Every prior agency had tried to teach
them a CMS. None of them succeeded.

The brief I took on was simple: **make the website disappear**. They
shouldn't have to think about it. The team should be able to update menus
in the time it takes to brew coffee, and customers should be able to book
a table without scrolling.
