The Fleetbase team came to me already knowing what was wrong: their site
read like a brochure. They wanted it to read like a product demo.

So we cut. Hard. Three pages of "trusted by industry leaders" copy
collapsed into a single line. Six feature pages collapsed into one.
Every section that survived had to earn its place by either showing the
product or shortening the path to a demo request.
